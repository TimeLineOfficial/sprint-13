Phase 2: 3NF Relational Database Schema & Proofs

Complete normalization guarantees zero storage anomalies and concurrency safety.

1NF (First Normal Form)

Every attribute contains strictly atomic, scalar values. Repeating groups (e.g., intermediate stops) are decomposed into the dedicated WAYPOINTS table.

2NF (Second Normal Form)

All non-key attributes depend on the entire primary key. Composite key partial dependencies eliminated via surrogate UUIDs in VEHICLES and DRIVERS.

3NF (Third Normal Form)

Transitive functional dependencies ($X \to Y \to Z$) strictly removed. Driver phone numbers and organization tiers isolated to their respective entities.
